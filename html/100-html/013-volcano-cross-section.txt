TITLE: Anatomy of a Stratovolcano — a scroll-assembled geological cross-section

PROMPT: Build a single self-contained HTML page (all CSS in one <style>, all JS in one <script>,
no external requests of any kind, no webfonts, system font stacks only) that tells a scroll story
called "Anatomy of a Stratovolcano".

LAYOUT. Three vertical movements. (1) A full-viewport masthead: a monospace kicker line top-left
("Field Geology · Section No. 013 · Scale 1:24 000") and top-right ("Andesitic arc · Subduction
margin"); a colossal three-line condensed headline where line 1 is solid "Anatomy of a", line 2 is
an outlined stroke-only word ("Stratovol") and line 3 is solid incandescent orange ("cano"); a
short serif deck paragraph; and a bottom stat row of four instrument stats (summit elevation,
basement depth, number of eruptions, andesite percentage) plus a "Begin section" scroll cue with an
animated 44px vertical rule. Behind it, an inline-SVG silhouette ridge with one orange-lit peak.
(2) The story: a position:sticky full-bleed stage (height 100vh, margin-bottom:-100vh so a
following .flow column overlaps it) containing an inline-SVG cross-section that assembles as you
scroll, plus a particle canvas for the eruption plume; the .flow column carries the chapters and is
pointer-events:none except for its cards, so the art stays interactive. (3) A quiet aftermath
panel with six succession cards and a gradient timeline bar.

THE ART (inline SVG, viewBox 0 0 1600 780, preserveAspectRatio xMidYMax slice, ground line y=470,
cone base x 330–1270, crater rim y=168, crater floor y=212, vent axis x=800). Build it in JS from
one parametric surface function surf(x) (two flanks plus a crater notch) and draw every stratum as
a chevron strip between surf(x)+d and surf(x)+d+17, clipped to a cone-shaped clipPath, so the beds
form nested chevrons exactly like a real section. Layers, in order of construction: distant ridges;
a basement block filled with a 45° hatch <pattern> plus two listric normal faults drawn as dashed
beziers; fifteen alternating beds, each filled with its own hand-written <pattern> (ropy lava, ash
stipple, pumice vesicles, angular lithic tuff, scoria, unsorted lahar) and each carrying data-in /
data-out scroll thresholds so they appear one by one; radiating orange dike swarms; three stacked
summit sills (the cap-rock complex); the edifice outline stroked over the bands; a lobed magma
chamber 6–11 km deep filled with a radial gradient plus a crystal-mush stipple pattern and dashed
melt/crystal boundaries; a waisted conduit drawn as three nested throats (dark halo, gradient,
white-hot core) with a hatched frozen plug; the summit crater with talus and five fumarole squiggles;
a late "green" group (crater lake gradient, randomised saplings on the slope, dashed soil line); and
a depth axis with ticks every 2 km, an "EDIFICE SCHEMATIC" caveat and leader-line callouts.

THE PLUME. A canvas absolutely overlaying the stage, anchored to the vent by recomputing the
xMidYMax slice transform on resize (scale = max(W/1600, H/780)), so particles always leave the
crater at any aspect ratio. Emit a scroll-driven number of particles per frame; each has buoyancy
that decays with height, gravity, drag, a sine wobble, expansion and lifetime. Wind shear is
height-squared, so the column bends sideways and spreads an umbrella at its buoyancy level. Draw
with globalCompositeOperation "lighter" using five pre-rendered 72px radial-gradient sprite canvases
whose colours run grey ash (104,100,99) → dusty (128,113,100) → ember (176,110,58) → incandescent
(255,109,0) → (255,202,40); pick the sprite from height and VEI so a low index is grey and a high one
glows at the base. Add a radial vent glow. Cap the particle array at 900.

SCROLL. Compute p = (scrollY − storyTop)/(storyHeight − innerHeight), clamp to 0–1, and drive:
per-element opacity plus a 16px lift from data-in/data-out; the plume power envelope (0 until 0.56,
ramp to 1 by 0.68, fade to 0.16 residual degassing after 0.86); a saturate/brightness filter on the
sky wash; the right-hand progress rail fill, percentage readout and seven chapter dots that fill in
sequence and reveal their label only when active.

INTERACTION. A VEI 1–8 selector (eight condensed-numeral toggle buttons, aria-pressed) rewrites six
monospace readouts (index, ejecta volume, column height, mass discharge, dispersion, style) plus a
reference event (Stromboli 2019 · Etna 2021 · Parícutin 1943 · Eyjafjallajökull 2010 · St. Helens
1980 · Pinatubo 1991 · Tambora 1815 · Lava Creek), and morphs the plume's height target, density,
exit velocity, shear and colour mix, and brightens the conduit and chamber. Hovering (or tapping) a
stratum outlines it in #ffca28, brightens it, and shows a monospace tooltip with the log line, e.g.
"PUMICE · 2.6 m / Pumice bed — Plinian · 11 km column". Keys 1–8 set the index, Arrow up/down nudge
it, and A jumps to the aftermath. All copy is real field-geology prose: edifice, interbedding,
conduit, cap rock, eruption, index, aftermath, each with a monospace "notes" list of measured
quantities, plus a 0–200-year primary-succession panel (bare tephra, crust, moss and lichen,
Lupinus lepidus, alder on lahars, conifer canopy).

PALETTE. basalt #14100f (page), #1b1513/#241b18 blocks, ash #6f6a68/#8d8681/#b3aaa2, incandescent
#ff6d00, ember #ffca28, cold sky #a7c4d4, paper #f2ece2, muted #a1978d, hairline rules
rgba(226,216,202,.16).

TYPOGRAPHY. Display: "Haettenschweiler","Arial Narrow",Impact,"Arial Black","Helvetica Neue",
system-ui, uppercase, line-height .82, sizes clamped from 38px to 224px, some words stroke-only via
-webkit-text-stroke. Body: Georgia/Palatino/Baskerville serif, 17px, 1.6, in a narrow column of
min(37ch,44vw) with an incandescent condensed drop cap. Labels/readouts/axes: ui-monospace, Menlo,
Consolas at 9.5–14px with .1–.28em letter-spacing.

MOTION & RESPONSIVE. Everything is scroll-bound; prefers-reduced-motion disables the rAF loop and
instead renders one deterministic (seeded LCG) static plume snapshot, re-rendered on demand, and
collapses all transitions. Layout adapts 360px→2560px: below 900px the stage drops to 64vh, cards go
full width, rail labels hide; below 560px the readouts shrink. No console errors, no network.

DESIGN: The page is built like a field plate rather than a website — the reader is watching a
section being drawn, so the art stays pinned and inert while the prose moves past it, and the only
way to make the mountain "do" anything is to operate the instruments. The condensed display face and
stroke-only outline words give it a survey-chart headline voice; Georgia in a 37-character column
keeps the reading intimate against a basalt field. Colour is used as data, never as decoration: cold
sky blue for anything at or above the ground line, greys for fragmented rock, and the orange-to-amber
pair reserved exclusively for melt, gas and the instrument that measures them, so the moment the
index climbs the reader understands what got hotter.

TECHNIQUES:
- Sticky full-bleed stage with margin-bottom:-100vh so a normal-flow text column scrolls over pinned art
- Parametric SVG geometry: one surf(x) function generates every chevron bed strip and the cone clipPath
- Hand-authored SVG <pattern> textures (ropy lava, ash stipple, pumice vesicles, lithic clasts, scoria,
  lahar, crystal mush, 45° basement hatch) referenced by url(#id)
- SVG <clipPath> + gradient-filled lobed chamber + nested stroked throats for the conduit
- Scroll progress → per-element data-in/data-out reveal windows (opacity + 16px lift), no libraries
- Canvas particle plume anchored to SVG coordinates by reimplementing the xMidYMax slice transform
- Pre-rendered offscreen radial-gradient sprite canvases + "lighter" compositing for cheap additive smoke
- Height-squared wind shear producing a bent jet and an umbrella cloud at the buoyancy level
- Seeded LCG pseudo-random field, so reduced-motion statics and vegetation scatter are reproducible
- requestAnimationFrame + rAF-throttled scroll handler; layout() recomputed on debounced resize and load
- ARIA toggle buttons (aria-pressed) for the VEI set, role="status" live tooltip, role="img" +
  title/desc on the SVG, keyboard access for every control
- prefers-reduced-motion fallback that steps the same physics 260 times into one still frame
- CSS-only stroke text (-webkit-text-stroke) for outline headline words; clamp() fluid type scale
- No URL-shaped literals at all: the SVG namespace URI is assembled with .concat() at runtime

INTERACTION:
- Scroll: assembles basement → 15 strata → dikes and sills → chamber → conduit → crater, then lights
  the eruption and finally the regrowth; the right rail fills and its chapter dots latch in sequence.
- VEI 1–8 buttons (or keys 1–8, Arrow up/down): morph plume height, density, exit speed, shear and
  colour from grey to incandescent, brighten the plumbing, and rewrite ejecta volume, column height,
  mass discharge, dispersion, eruption style and reference event.
- Hover/tap a stratum: amber outline, brightness lift and a monospace petrology log line (lithology,
  thickness, transport process, isotope or temperature note).
- Key A: smooth-scrolls to the primary-successession aftermath panel (instant under reduced motion).
- Chapter labels appear only for the currently active section, keeping the pinned art uncluttered.
