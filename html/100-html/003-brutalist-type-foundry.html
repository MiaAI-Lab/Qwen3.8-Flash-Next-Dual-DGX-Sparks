01 / Manifesto

Rules,
not ornaments

Four laws. They are printed on the wall of the studio and nobody has ever taken them down.

We started STRIKE in a print basement with a guillotine that jammed and a scanner held together with tape. Everything we draw since has to pass one test: does it still read from thirty metres, on wet card, at night?

A typeface is not a mood board. It is a machine for making a sentence unavoidable. So we remove the parts you were going to admire and keep the parts that do the shouting.

The grid is visible because the grid is honest. Cover it up and you are just decorating an argument.

Law I — The rule is the decoration

Every border on this page is 4px. Every shadow is a flat offset. Zero blur, zero radius, zero gradient. If it can be drawn with a straight edge, draw it with a straight edge.

Law II — Contrast is a volume knob

Paper #F2EFE6 against ink #0D0D0D. Acid #D8FF00 only where a decision is required. Alarm #FF3B1F only where a decision was wrong.

Law III — Weight before colour

If a headline fails at 900 it will not be saved by a hue. Set it heavier, tighter, closer to the edge of the sheet — then stop.

Law IV — Ship the ugly draft

STRIKE GROTESK NO.1 shipped with three kerning pairs. It has been downloaded 41 908 times. Polish later. Speak now.
