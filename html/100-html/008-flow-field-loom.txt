TITLE: Flow Field Loom — a generative weaving studio that accumulates thousands of translucent threads along a hand-written fBm curl field

PROMPT: Build a single self-contained page (one HTML file, all CSS in one <style>, all JS in one <script>, zero external references — no CDN, no web fonts, no images, no libraries, offline) that fills the viewport with a full-bleed <canvas> which is never cleared: thousands of short translucent strokes are laid along a drifting flow field and accumulate into a cloth. Everything is drawn from your own 2D value noise + fBm written in plain JS.

Typography: three system stacks only — a monospace for every label and readout (ui-monospace, SFMono-Regular, Menlo, Consolas, "Courier New"), a geometric display for the masthead (Futura, "Avenir Next", "Century Gothic", "Helvetica Neue", system-ui, weight 700, tracked .2em, uppercase), and a high-contrast serif for the one italic word in the title and the wall-label headline (Didot, "Bodoni MT", Baskerville, Palatino, Georgia). Default ground #0b0d12, ink #e9e6dd, accent brass #c8a24a, panel rgba(13,15,20,.72), hairlines rgba(233,230,221,.16/.3).

Layout, all floating above the canvas: #loom fixed inset:0 z-index 0; a .veil of three crossed repeating-linear-gradients (90deg/0deg/45deg, soft-light, opacity .55) that reads as woven fibre; a .vig radial vignette (transparent to 46%, rgba(0,0,0,.42) at the edge); a .frame of four L-shaped corner ticks in hairline borders. Top-left .mark: "FLOW FIELD" in the tracked display face with the word "loom" set in serif italic brass, and a micro-caps sub-line. Top-right .specimen, a museum wall label: translucent panel with a 2px brass top rule, a serif headline ("Untitled Weave Nº 8 — accumulation on a drifting field") and a two-column <dl> whose keys are 8.4px tracked uppercase micro-labels — Technique, Symmetry, Structure, Field, Threads, Dimensions — plus a hairline-topped foot line carrying the seed. Bottom-centre a slim floating .strip (panel + hairline + backdrop blur) of .cell blocks separated by 1px hairlines, each with a mono .lbl (numbered .n in brass, name, right-aligned .v value): 01 FIELD SCALE (range 40–520 px), 02 CURL (0–1), 03 STROKE ALPHA (.02–.60), 04 DENSITY (120–2400 threads), 05 WEAVE MODE segmented control SILK/TWILL/DENIM, 06 PALETTE row of four .chip swatches (each a .sw of four 25%-wide <i> blades with a name tooltip rising on hover, plus a native title because the strip clips that bubble once it becomes scrollable at ≤900px), 07 LOOM actions RE-WARP and PAUSE, then a .seed cell reading the live seed over a "N threads · M strokes" line. Above the strip a faint mono .hint that fades on first pointer contact; a light-on-dark .toast that drops in on every state change. Breakpoints at 1120/900/680/420/1900px: the strip wraps to 2-up then 1-up and becomes scrollable at 46vh, the wall label re-anchors under the masthead, sliders go full-width.

Noise and advection: hash the integer lattice with Math.imul (ix·374761393 + iy·668265263 + seed·1442695041, then ^>>>13 · 1274126177, then ^>>>16, /2^32), interpolate the four corner values with smoothstep for bilinear value noise, and stack 4 octaves (gain .5, lacunarity 2.03, per-octave offsets 17.31/−11.17) for fBm. Spatial frequency is F = 2.2/scale so FIELD SCALE is a real wavelength in pixels. Thread direction = a unit vector at angle fBm·4π, blended with curl(ψ) = (∂ψ/∂y, −∂ψ/∂x) of a second fBm potential measured by central differences at ε = max(1.5, scale·0.05); CURL is that blend factor, so at 1 the flow is divergence-free and folds instead of shearing. Time enters only as drifting domain offsets (t·0.55 / −t·0.3), never as a third noise dimension. Then bias the direction toward the nearest loom axis by |dot| — warp/weft for SILK (20%), the ±45° rib for TWILL (55%), a 3/1 rib for DENIM (42%) — so the accumulation reads as woven, not marbled.

Threads and strokes: a pooled array of particles; 40% spawn on quantised vertical warp columns (cols = max(8, W/26)), the rest uniformly. Colour is hsl picked from one of two hue bands per palette, with an "ash" probability that drops saturation to 4–13%. Per segment: alpha = STROKE_ALPHA × modeAlpha × glow(0.72–1.32) × sin(πt)^0.42, which tapers both ends so threads fray instead of stopping dead; perpendicular jitter comes from a cheap noise sample; a stroke is one beginPath/moveTo/lineTo/stroke. WEAVE MODE genuinely changes the physics: SILK speed 2.6 px/frame in 2 sub-steps, life 70–190 (180–490 px threads), width .5–1.2, alpha ×.34, jitter .5, globalCompositeOperation "screen". TWILL speed 1.25, life 7–18 (9–22 px diagonal dashes), width .7–1.6, alpha ×.90, jitter 1.15, composite "overlay". DENIM speed 1.8, life 10–30 (18–54 px), width 1.2–2.6, alpha ×.85, jitter 1.9, setLineDash([6,9]), composite "source-over" on a light ground.

Palettes, each with its own ground and hue bands: "Indigo Dusk" ground #0b0d12, hues 228±16 and 268±20, sat 44–88, lig 36–74, 14% ash, accent #c8a24a · "Ash & Ember" ground #100e0d, hues 13±12 and 38±15, sat 40–92, lig 34–70, 24% ash, accent #e2743a · "Sea Glass" ground #08130f, hues 162±22 and 196±20, sat 28–72, lig 42–78, 12% ash, accent #84d6bd · "Kobo" ground #0d0e12, hues 344±14 and 42±16, sat 34–82, lig 32–68, 18% ash, accent #d8b26a. DENIM swaps the ground for hsl(bandHue, 14%, 87%) and inverts the UI: --ink #171a20, --ink-inv #f3f1e9, panel rgba(246,244,238,.74), accent shaded to 62%, meta color-scheme flipped to light. Changing palette or ground pushes values into the CSS custom properties (--bg, --ink, --ink-inv, --dim, --faint, --hair, --hair2, --accent, --panel) so the whole interface relights through one .9s body transition, with no second stylesheet.

Motion and framing: the weave grows continuously; the pointer is a repulsion vortex that parts the threads; RE-WARP washes the cloth out with a sweeping beater comb and restarts from a new seed; PAUSE cancels the animation frame entirely. devicePixelRatio is clamped to 2.5, the context is scaled with setTransform so all geometry stays in CSS pixels, and a resize snapshots the current cloth into a scratch canvas and stretches it back over the new backing store so the accumulation survives. A work-time governor holds roughly 1600 strokes/frame at 1440×900@2× and scales the ceiling by W·H·DPR². prefers-reduced-motion must draw one composed static weave (20 rAF chunks × 3 thread frames, ≈190k segments) and never start the loop.

DESIGN: The page is a conservation studio rather than an app: one dark rectangular field, a hairline-framed loom, and a wall label that describes the thing as though it had already been acquired. All of the colour belongs to the cloth, and the interface is deliberately mute — brushed-glass panels, brass numerals, 8.6px tracked monospace — so the only saturated hues are the threads themselves. The weave mode is treated as a material decision, not a filter: SILK is a long-floated satin that glows additively, TWILL is a short diagonal rib built from opaque flecks, and DENIM turns the whole room light because indigo yarn is read on a pale warp. The museum copy commits to the fiction (technique, symmetry, dimensions), the seed is treated like an edition number, and RE-WARP borrows the vocabulary of the loom — the beater comb sweeping the shed — so a hard reset still feels like one more pass of weaving rather than a page reload.

TECHNIQUES:
- hand-written integer-lattice value noise (Math.imul hash, smoothstep bilinear blend) + 4-octave fBm with lacunarity 2.03 and per-octave domain offsets — nothing imported
- real curl noise: central-difference gradient of an fBm potential, rotated (∂ψ/∂y, −∂ψ/∂x) and lerped against a raw angle layer, so CURL blends a divergence-free field into the flow
- time evolution through drifting domain offsets only (2D noise stays 2D)
- loom-axis snapping: nearest of two bias vectors chosen by |dot product|, lerped by a per-mode strength, which is what makes the accumulation read as warp and weft rather than marble
- additive accumulation on a never-cleared, alpha:false canvas with globalCompositeOperation switched per weave mode ("screen" / "overlay" / "source-over")
- per-segment sin(πt)^0.42 alpha taper to fray both ends of every thread; perpendicular jitter sampled from the same noise so it is coherent, not random
- setLineDash([6,9]) coarse strokes plus a light ground and inverted ink tokens for DENIM
- CSS custom properties as the relighting API between JS and the existing stylesheet (--bg/--ink/--ink-inv/--dim/--faint/--hair/--hair2/--accent/--panel), plus one JS-set --ink-inv so pressed pills, chips and the toast stay legible on both grounds
- meta color-scheme flipped dark↔light at runtime so form controls and scrollbars follow the DENIM ground
- devicePixelRatio-aware backing store (DPR clamped to 2.5) with ctx.setTransform, and an offscreen snapshot/redraw so the accumulated cloth survives a DPR-correct resize
- a 60fps budget that times the work itself (ema of performance.now() around the thread pass), not the frame delta, and multiplies the stroke ceiling by viewport area × DPR² — the thread count is the throttle, so quality degrades before frames do
- 24-bit seed driving both mulberry32 spawn order and the noise lattice key (seed ^ 0x9e3779b9), printed as a 5-character base-36 edition number
- RE-WARP wipe: ground wash whose alpha ramps with progress, crossed by a createLinearGradient comb band sweeping the full width, while the live threads fade out under a global fade multiplier
- fibre texture and vignette as pure crossed repeating-linear-gradients under mix-blend-mode: soft-light, instead of an image
- backdrop-filter blur+saturate panels, hairline-separated flex cells, gradient-free 4-blade CSS swatch chips built from a data table in JS
- prefers-reduced-motion branch that composes one static weave in rAF chunks and never opens the loop, with controls re-composing it on a 240ms debounce

INTERACTION:
- FIELD SCALE retunes the noise wavelength in live pixels (40–520), CURL blends toward the divergence-free field, STROKE ALPHA scales every segment, DENSITY sets the live thread count — all four apply instantly to the weave in progress, no restart
- WEAVE MODE re-sets stroke length, width, jitter, dash and composite operation; because DENIM also changes the ground, switching to or from it triggers a full re-warp so the cloth is rebuilt on the new ground rather than painted over the old one
- The pointer is a repulsion vortex: within 2.2 × radius (radius = clamp(80, 240, min(W,H)·0.19)) the flow is pushed radially outward and swirled tangentially with a smoothstep falloff, which clears a measurable hole in the accumulation (−83% stroke coverage at the core) and strands a bright rim at 0.82 × radius by lifting stroke lightness and alpha. Pointerdown/up injects a decaying burst (+1.1, ×0.9 per frame) for a visible shove
- The vortex eases in with pointer motion and fades 1.5s after the pointer stops or leaves the window, so the cloth closes back over
- 1–4 select the palette, M cycles SILK→TWILL→DENIM, R re-warps, Space toggles PAUSE; keys are ignored while focus is inside a slider so arrow-key stepping still works
- RE-WARP plays the ~980ms comb wipe, then reseeds from a fresh 24-bit value; the new seed appears simultaneously in the strip readout and the museum foot line, and a toast names it
- PAUSE cancels requestAnimationFrame outright (zero strokes while paused) and relabels to RESUME; returning to a hidden tab suspends the loop and resumes it without a time-jump
- Every palette/mode/seed change reports through a drop-in toast and the aria-live region; chips, segmented pills and actions expose aria-pressed; the canvas carries role="img" and a label, and a .sr-only noscript line covers the no-JS case
- Resize (150ms debounce) rebuilds the backing store, re-scales the vortex radius and the stroke ceiling for the new pixel load, and preserves the existing cloth by stretching it — the hint line auto-fades after first pointer contact or 12s
