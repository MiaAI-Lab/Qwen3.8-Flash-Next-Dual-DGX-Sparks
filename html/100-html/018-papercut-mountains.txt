TITLE: Ninth Range — A Layered Paper Diorama

PROMPT: Build a single self-contained page (one HTML file, all CSS in one <style>, all JS in one <script>, no external assets, system fonts only) that renders a stacked paper-cut mountain diorama filling the viewport, with a time-of-day control that relights the whole stack. Fonts: soft geometric display from the stack Futura, "Avenir Next", Optima, "Trebuchet MS", "Helvetica Neue", system-ui; ui-monospace/Menlo for data and labels; Hoefler Text/Palatino/Georgia italic for one aphorism. Page background #140f1e, paper ink #fdf3ea.

Layout: a .scroller of 255vh containing a position:sticky 100svh .viewport with perspective 1300px, perspective-origin 50% 48% and isolation:isolate. Inside, in painting order: a flat .sky gradient (linear 180deg var(--sky-a) → var(--sky-b) 52% → var(--sky-c)) with a screen-blended radial sun bloom tracking var(--sun-x)/var(--sun-y); a JS-generated .stars field of 110 2–3px dots with random twinkle delays at translateZ(-820px); then .diorama, a preserve-3d plane holding every paper sheet; then flat atmosphere overlays (.haze screen blend, radial .vignette, a .grain made of two repeating-linear-gradients plus a 4px radial-dot background-size, and a 2px top .seam). A .ui grid overlay (pointer-events:none, re-enabled on children) carries the masthead, the clock control, the legend, the credit block, a top-centre camera-depth readout and a bottom-centre scroll cue.

Sheets: thirteen depth planes, each an inline SVG positioned inset:0 with viewBox "0 0 1000 600" and preserveAspectRatio="xMidYMax slice", each carrying inline style --z (depth), --k (perspective compensation scale) and --pf (parallax factor), and transform: translate3d(calc(var(--px)*var(--pf)*1px), calc(var(--py)*var(--pf)*.42px), var(--z)) scale(var(--k)) so that apparent size is exactly 1 at rest. Depths: cloud high −700, far ridge −660, second ridge −580, cloud middle −540, shoulder ridge −500, low ridge −420, first fir line −340, mist slit band −300, second fir line −260, birds −160, meadow & cabin −180, near hill −110, foreground cut −40; k = (1300+|z|)/1300. Each ridge sheet is one <path> in that SVG's <defs>, reused five times: a .cast copy (fill var(--cut), translateY(-15), blur(9px)) drawn first so the paper covers its lower half and the shadow lands on the sheet behind; a .hi copy (stroke var(--edge) 2.4, translateY(-1.3)) giving the crisp 1px lighter top edge; .pp (fill var(--lN)); .tex (fill url(#tooth), a 6×6 rotated pattern of hairlines and dots for paper tooth); .sh (fill url(#depth), a vertical gradient white .10 → transparent → black .30). Ridges 1–4 and 7–9 are hand-plotted polylines/curves; the two fir lines are sawtooth treelines built from repeated relative segments (16-30 16 30 14-20 14 20 18-36 18 36 12-22 12 22 and a smaller variant) long enough to overpass the viewBox. The sun is a paper disc div at translateZ(-760px) scale(1.585) with a radial-gradient body (var(--sun-c) → var(--sun-c2)), a screen halo ::before at var(--sun-halo) and an overlay stripe texture ::after. Clouds are slit lenses (one shared <path id="slitP"> plus a <g id="slit"> group of cast/edge/paper copies) inside <g class="band"> groups drifting on linear CSS keyframes at 158s / 112s / 78s. Three birds ride closed bezier loops via <animateMotion> (38s, staggered with negative begin) inside a wrapper g so a nested g can carry scale and each wing can flap independently with a scaleY(1 → .18) keyframe at .52s alternate, transform-box:view-box and transform-origin:0 0. The meadow sheet carries a cabin: dark cut walls, roof and chimney, two firs, a 1px lit roof line and two warm windows (fill #ffcf7a at opacity var(--win)) with a blurred .winglow ellipse behind them.

Type overlay: eyebrow "Sheet study № 18 · nine cuts, one horizon" in 0.58rem monospace tracked .36em; h1 "Ninth Range" at clamp(2.1rem,7vw,5.1rem), weight 300, tracked .06em, uppercase, with an italic serif sub-line at 0.3em; a 34ch lede; a "PAPER STUDIO — layered diorama, 2026" credit block with a 52px rule and an italic note about 300 gsm cotton stock and a No. 11 blade; a legend naming all thirteen sheets with a colour swatch (bound to the live var(--lN) so it re-tints with the light) and its depth in mm.

Lighting: four hand-mixed settings — Dawn (sky #3a2c4a → #b96a6a → #f6c9a8, sheets #b08a9b → #1b1326, low big sun at 22%/62% r126 in #ffd9a8/#e08a4e, stars .10, windows .18), Midday (#7fb0d0 → #bfd9e4 → #eef3ec, cool green-grey sheets #b8c6c9 → #22332a, small high white sun 64%/16% r96, no stars or windows, brighter clouds), Dusk (#2e2340 → #8a4a5e → #f2a06a, sheets #a46a72 → #130e18, huge low sun 79%/56% r138 in #ffb26b/#e2603a, stars .20, windows .50) and Night (#05070f → #121a30 → #22304a, blue-black sheets #2b3552 → #05070c, a small pale moon 30%/20% r56, stars 1, cabin windows 1). Each setting also carries haze, edge-light, cast-shadow, cloud and bird colours. Switching runs a 1600ms requestAnimationFrame tween with an easeInOutCubic curve, interpolating every channel of every colour (hex and rgba are both parsed into [r,g,b,a] arrays) plus sunX/sunY/sunR/star/win/rim/cloudO/halo, and writing them back as CSS custom properties on :root — so sky, every sheet, the rim light, the shadow tint, the sun, clouds, birds, star opacity, cabin glow and the legend swatches all move together.

Interaction: pointer movement across the viewport sets a target −1…1 vector eased at 6% per frame, published once per frame as --px/--py on .diorama; each sheet multiplies it by its own --pf so near sheets travel up to 54px while the far ridge barely shifts, and the whole plane also takes rotateX(−4deg)/rotateY(6deg) — never more than about 6deg — with the sun and star field counter-parallaxing at their own tiny factors. Scrolling pushes the camera deeper: scroll progress is eased and applied as translateZ up to 400px plus a 5% scale on the diorama plane, with a "Camera depth n mm" readout updated in 5mm steps and the haze lifting as you go. The Time of Day control is a real radiogroup (four pill buttons, roving tabindex, arrow-key support, aria-checked) driving the tween. Under prefers-reduced-motion all keyframe animation collapses, the birds' SMIL is paused via pauseAnimations(), the tilt and parallax loop are dropped, the camera follows scroll directly, the scroller collapses to 100vh and time switches snap. Responsive from 360px (stacked masthead, horizontally scrollable one-row legend, hidden lede and credit) through 2560px (larger title, wider fan of type).

DESIGN: The page is built like the object it depicts: thirteen flat sheets, each a single silhouette with one lit top edge and one cast shadow, stacked in Z so that depth is a physical property rather than a gradient. The palette is deliberately restrained — a hand-mixed set of paper colours per hour, from dawn's #f6c9a8 / #b96a6a / #3a2c4a through night's blue-blacks — so the whole diorama can be re-lit without ever changing shape, and the legend's swatches re-tint along with it to keep the fiction honest. Typography is quiet and geometric, set wide and small in monospace for anything factual, so the paper does the talking; motion is slow and mechanical (clouds at 78–158 seconds, birds on a 38-second loop) because paper should feel moved, not animated. Interaction reinforces the metaphor: you lean over the stack rather than scrolling a story, and the only narrative control offered is the hour of day.

TECHNIQUES:
- real 3D sheet stacking: perspective on the viewport, transform-style:preserve-3d on the plane, per-sheet translateZ with a precomputed k = (P+d)/P compensation scale so every sheet is exactly viewport-sized at rest
- parallax published once per frame as two inherited custom properties (--px/--py) that each sheet multiplies by its own depth factor --pf, so the JS touches one element per frame, not thirteen
- paper edge physics per sheet: cast copy translated up and blurred (shadow onto the sheet behind), a 1px lighter .hi stroke offset −1.3, solid fill, a tooth <pattern> fill and a top-to-bottom depth gradient fill — all <use> references of one <path> in defs
- preserveAspectRatio="xMidYMax slice" so every sheet covers any viewport while its cut edge stays anchored to the bottom
- sawtooth treelines written as repeated relative path segments; hand-plotted absolute polylines for rock ridges; smooth C/S curves for meadow and foreground cuts
- time-of-day engine: hex + rgba both parsed to [r,g,b,a], every channel linearly interpolated over 1600ms with easeInOutCubic, written back as CSS custom properties on :root
- slit paper clouds as one shared <path>/<g> in defs reused through <use>, drifting on CSS keyframes at three different rates; birds on closed-bezier <animateMotion> with nested wrapper groups so SMIL motion, scale and CSS wing-flap never fight over one transform
- cabin windows as a night-only reveal: fill at opacity var(--win) plus a blurred warm ellipse behind
- scroll-driven camera: sticky 100svh viewport inside a 255vh scroller, progress eased into translateZ + scale on the diorama plane
- texture from layered CSS gradients only (two repeating-linear-gradients and a 4px radial-dot background-size for grain), zero images
- accessible radiogroup control with roving tabindex and arrow keys; prefers-reduced-motion branch that pauses SMIL, drops the rAF tilt loop and snaps colour changes

INTERACTION:
- Pointer movement parallaxes all thirteen sheets by depth (foreground up to ~54px, far ridge ~4px) and tilts the entire plane up to 6deg with 6% per-frame easing; leaving the window eases the stack back to rest
- Scrolling walks the camera into the diorama (translateZ to 400px + 5% scale), reported as a live "Camera depth n mm" readout, while the inter-sheet haze strengthens
- Time of Day (Dawn / Midday / Dusk / Night) cross-fades every layer colour, the sun's position, size and hue, the rim light, the shadow tint, cloud tone, bird colour, star opacity and the cabin's window glow over 1.6s on an easeInOutCubic curve; keyboard-operable via arrows within the radiogroup
- The legend doubles as a depth key: every sheet named, swatched from its live colour variable and stamped with its distance in millimetres
- Continuous ambient interaction only — cloud bands drifting at three rates, three birds on staggered 38s bezier loops with a 0.52s wing flap, stars twinkling — all of which stop under prefers-reduced-motion, where the camera instead follows scroll one-to-one and time changes apply instantly
