TITLE: Nocturne N-14 — a single watch, drawn not photographed (luxury chronometry one-product page)

PROMPT: Build a fully self-contained, offline, single-page showcase for one fictional luxury watch, the "Nocturne N-14" by "Maison Nocturne, Genève". Palette: near-black #0b0b0c ground, warm white #f5f2ec text, champagne #c9a97a accents, blued-steel #3a5bbf/#7d9aee hands. Ending: a two-column notes section on the escapement and the free-sprung balance, then a centred "Enquire" section whose hairline underline wipes in on hover (transform:scaleX), and a mono footer. Everything responsive from 360px (single column, sticky short stage, leaders hidden) to 2560px.

DESIGN: The page behaves like a museum vitrine: near-black ground, warm-white Didot titles, champagne hairlines, and generous negative space that forces all attention onto one object. The watch is the entire brand — drawn with light instead of photography, so it can be re-drawn at any size, taken apart on scroll, and set to the visitor's own time, which quietly converts a product shot into an instrument that is "yours, now". Copy is restrained and factual-craft (temperatures, passes of the cutting tool, poising screws), the spec rail speaks in tabular mono like a technical drawing, and the closing notes on the escapement end the page the way a watchmaker's letter would: slowly, and with an enquire line you have to arrive at.
