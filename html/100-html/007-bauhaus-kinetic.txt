TITLE: Bauhaus Kinetic — Poster Composer Nº 7

PROMPT: Build a single self-contained HTML page that is a working Bauhaus poster composer, not a static mock-up. Everything inline (one <style>, one <script>, no external requests, system fonts only).

INK + PAPER: paper #f4f1e8, red #d62828, blue #003049, yellow #fcbf49, ink #111111. Flat solids only — no gradients inside the shapes, no drop shadows on the geometry. The page body carries a paper "tooth" built from stacked repeating gradients: two soft radial washes (highlight top-left, shade bottom-right), a 3px horizontal fibre repeating-linear-gradient at 3% ink, a 4px vertical fibre at 2.4% ink, 45° and −45° hairline hatchings tinted red and blue at 2%, and one 640px vertical rule at 16% red. Above everything sits a fixed, pointer-events:none "grain" plate (mix-blend-mode:multiply, opacity .5) of three repeating-conic-gradients plus two 3px repeating-linear-gradients, and a subtle radial vignette.

TYPE: display stack "Futura","Avenir Next","Century Gothic","Helvetica Neue",system-ui,sans-serif at weight 700 with tight caps tracking (letter-spacing −.045em on the first headline line, +.004em on the second); body Trebuchet MS; labels in ui-monospace/SFMono/Menlo. Headline is split over two lines: "BAUHAUS" in ink and "1919—2026" in red, with a solid blue rule (height .075em) between them. Both lines must be auto-fitted: keep hidden measurement spans (same face, same em tracking, font-size 100px) off-canvas, measure their width, then solve font-size = target / measured × 100 and clamp by plate height, so the headline always spans the plate exactly. Add a 90° side rail down the left edge (writing-mode:vertical-rl + rotate(180deg), .34em tracking): "Form — Farbe — Bewegung · Weimar · Dessau · Berlin" with ink caps at both ends, plus small mono edition marks. Bottom of the plate: numbered caption block (01–04, mono, hanging red numbers) on the left, right-aligned mono colophon (technique, edition, French month/date stamp) on the right, separated from the plate by a hairline.

GRID / LAYOUT: CSS grid — left rail (28–56px) + poster, toolbar spanning the bottom (118–196px), gutter clamp(12px,2.6vw,34px). Poster keeps a hairline ink border, an inner highlight, a deep soft shadow, and touch-action:none. Under 820px the toolbar becomes sticky and the page scrolls; under 600px the rail collapses and the toolbar stacks. Above 1700px grow the gutter and caption size.

CONSTRUCTION GUIDES (toggleable, on by default): an inline SVG sized to the plate in real pixels (re-set its viewBox on every resize so strokes and text are never distorted) drawn with vector-effect="non-scaling-stroke" and 3/4px dashes: quarter lines, the 1/φ = .618 vertical and horizontal (the horizontal one in red), both reciprocal diagonals, a tinted φ cell, the inscribed circle + its square on the left edge, a φ-radius arc, 24 edge ticks per side with taller ticks every 6th, and live mono labels ("BLATT 1240×780 px", "1 / φ = 766", "φ = 1.6180", "0 · 1 · 1.618").

GEOMETRY: six kinds, each one path in a 0 0 100 100 viewBox rendered with preserveAspectRatio="none" into a box whose height = width × ratio: circle (r 1), half-disc (r .5), quarter arc (r 1), triangle (r .87), bar (r .26), ring (r 1, evenodd-free double path). Ten hand-tuned seed shapes placed asymmetrically as fractions of the plate (a large blue quarter bleeding off the top-left, a red circle at .585/.07, an ink bar rotated 90° at .03/.455, a yellow half-disc upside down at .395/.56, an ink triangle at 15° at .735/.47, a small red ring, a small ink dot, a blue bar at 4°, a yellow quarter at 90°, an ink half-disc at 90°) — nothing dead centre.

MISREGISTRATION: every shape is a <button class="shape"> containing two stacked spans: .face (the plate) and .ghost, a duplicate at 34% opacity, mix-blend-mode:multiply, offset 2px along a per-shape random phase (−2…+2px on both axes). Ghost ink is the second colour of each pair (red→blue, blue→yellow, yellow→red, ink→red, paper→ink). On hover/focus/drag the offset triples and opacity rises to .62 — the plate "prints high".

INTERACTION: Pointer Events only (mouse, pen and touch through), pointerdown on the layer → capture on the shape, pointermove writes CSS custom properties --x/--y, pointerup/cancel/blur release; a 3px squared threshold distinguishes click from drag; positions clamp so at least 42% of a shape stays inside. Clicking empty plate deselects. Keyboard: arrows nudge 2px (12 with shift), alt+[ / alt+] scale, alt+, / alt+. rotate 15°, Delete removes and selects the neighbour. Toolbar groups numbered 01–06 in mono: six shape-add buttons (icon glyphs with German/German-English tooltips), five ink swatches (paper is a white square with a hairline border; active swatch gets an offset outline ring), size stepper (×1.14 per step, per-kind minimum), rotation stepper (±15°), COMPOSE, SHUFFLE, GUIDES toggle (aria-pressed), CLEAR, and a live readout (Blatt n/n, X·Y, grid state). Shortcuts C / S / G. A mono toast reports every action in a German/French register ("Konstruktionslinien ein", "Neu gesetzt — harte Schnitte"). A black ticker bar with a red top rule scrolls Bauhaus slogans with a duplicated track and translateX(−50%) keyframe.

ANIMATION: hand-written spring integrator on a single shared rAF loop — semi-implicit Euler, mass 1, k = 176, d = 15.2 for x/y/w and k = 150, d = 17 for rotation, four substeps per frame, dt clamped to 50ms, rest test on both error and velocity, transitions disabled while a shape is springing. COMPOSE sorts shapes by (x + 0.82y), assigns them to the four φ cells in the order [top-right, bottom-right, bottom-left, top-left], sizes each to fit its cell (min(cell.w×.94, cell.h/(r×1.04))), snaps rotation to the nearest 45°, and springs every property with a 55ms stagger — the underdamped spring supplies the overshoot. SHUFFLE does three hard cuts with no easing at all: full randomisation of size/position/rotation/ink on cut 1, then ±5% settles at 92ms and 78ms, class .is-cut suppressing transitions, a forced reflow between cuts so each cut actually paints. Entrance: the ten seed shapes start 34px left / 52px down at 70% size and spring in at 50ms intervals. prefers-reduced-motion disables the ticker, all transitions and every spring (COMPOSE then jumps instantly).

DESIGN: A letterpress plate that behaves like a tool. The page is drawn entirely from hairlines, dashed construction rules and flat primary solids on a warm, fibrous paper, so the composition reads as a working proof pinned to a studio bench rather than a website; typography is set by solving text width against plate width, and every interaction is answered with mono readouts and offset ghost ink. The only "effect" is mechanical: an underdamped spring and a 2px printing error, which is exactly the vocabulary of hand-fed lithography.

TECHNIQUES:
- CSS grid page frame with clamp()-driven gutter, rail width and toolbar height; three responsive breakpoints down to 360px and one up at 1700px
- Paper tooth and grain from layered repeating-linear/conic/radial gradients, no images; multiply blend plate over the whole page
- Text-to-width fitting via hidden 100px measurement spans with em-based tracking, re-solved on resize and on document.fonts.ready
- SVG construction grid re-viewBoxed to real plate pixels, vector-effect="non-scaling-stroke", dashed φ/armature rules with live numeric labels
- Shapes as <button> elements transformed through CSS custom properties (--x/--y/--a), so dragging never touches layout
- Misregistration via a duplicated low-opacity multiply ghost at a per-shape offset vector, amplified on hover/focus
- Shared requestAnimationFrame spring loop (semi-implicit Euler, substepping, velocity rest test) driving position, size and rotation simultaneously with a staggered start delay
- Golden-ratio cell solver for auto-composition with a curated cell visit order and per-cell fit limits
- Hard-cut shuffle: transition-suppressing class + forced reflow between timed randomisations
- Pointer Events with setPointerCapture for touch-correct dragging, squared-distance click threshold, blur/pointercancel cleanup
- prefers-reduced-motion honoured in CSS (ticker, transitions) and in JS (springs bypassed)

INTERACTION:
- Drag any shape with mouse, pen or finger; it lifts (z-order, cursor, ghost offset) and clamps to the plate
- Click to select: solid outline plus dashed selection frame, live X·Y and size/rotation readout
- Toolbar: add one of six forms, repaint with one of five inks, step size ×1.14, step rotation ±15°
- COMPOSE animates the whole plate onto a φ 1:1.618 grid with staggered spring overshoot; SHUFFLE re-scatters in three hard cuts
- GUIDES toggles the dashed construction armature (also keyboard G); CLEAR empties the plate; C / S keyboard equivalents
- Keyboard-only operation: arrows nudge, alt+brackets scale, alt+comma/period rotate, Delete removes and advances focus
- Hover or focus any form to see the second ink plate print high; ticker and toast narrate the studio state
